数据与一致性检查
Kvasir 验证集 120 张，CVC 评估集 612 张。8 模型共 5856 条逐图结果按数据集和文件名逐
一匹配；5856 份保存预测重算 Dice 与正式逐图值的绝对差均不超过 1e−6。分组加权均值还
原正式 Dice／IoU 均值，缺失或重复 ID 均会触发失败。

发现的指标命名问题
已检查的原始 SAM 与 PP-SAM 评估函数将最大 Hausdorff 距离写入名为 hd95 的字段，未计
算 95 百分位。旧字段保留为 legacy_distance，不再直接解释为 HD95。本轮另算双向表面距
离各自 95 百分位的较大值，单位为原图像素；边界 F1 容差为图像对角线的 0.5%，至少 1 像
素。
有 5 条空预测：边界 F1 记 0，表面 HD95 不定义，表中保留有效数量而非填 0。新指标与旧
距离不同，不能只修改表头后继续使用旧数值。

边界对比度探索分析
在真实边界内外分别取窄带，计算两个带状区域的平均 CIELAB 颜色距离。主分析带宽为图
像对角线的 0.5%，至少 2 像素。每个数据集按对比度 Q25 和 Q75 划分低、中、高组；分组
只使用图像与真实标注，不使用模型预测或得分。

代理指标的检查与限制




图 S3 每个数据集在中等面积组内选取对比度最低 2 张与最高 2 张；青色为真实轮廓。按图像特征选图，未按
预测好坏筛选。ID 见 boundary_proxy_example_ids.csv。

这些示例用于核查代理指标的含义，不能替代专家标注。均值颜色距离可能忽略局部模糊、
纹理和反光，出现颜色相消；也不能据此判断病理或临床难度。
尺度与颜色对比度存在相关：Spearman 相关系数在 Kvasir 为 0.2574，在 CVC 为 0.3707。已
输出尺度×对比度交叉分组表，但稀疏组不能支持可靠的独立效应结论。
敏感性检查使用 0.25%和 1%对角线带宽，以及真实边界上的灰度梯度中位数。CVC 图像的
0.25%与 0.5%带宽均因至少 2 像素规则取 2，二者不构成独立的带宽变化。CVC 的 MSA-L
在主对比度定义下高减低组 BF1 为−0.0231，在较宽带下为+0.0037，说明趋势依赖定义。
边界梯度分组中，两数据集的 8 模型均表现为高组平均 BF1 更高；该结果作为敏感性分析报
告，不因趋势更整齐而替换已记录的主代理定义。灰度梯度同样可能受纹理、反光和成像条
件影响。

可纳入论文的英文结果草稿
以下段落需与方法部分一同整合，并由作者核验。相应新表与图尚未编入正式论文章节号。

Performance across relative object sizes
Object scale was defined as the fraction of image pixels occupied by the ground-truth foreground.
Images were grouped into small (at most 5%), medium (more than 5% and at most 20%), and large
(more than 20%) foreground regions. The Kvasir validation groups contained 29, 61, and 30 images,
respectively, while the corresponding CVC groups contained 229, 321, and 62 images. These
categories describe relative image area rather than physical lesion dimensions.
The relationship between scale and overlap accuracy differed across models. On CVC, PP-SAM
achieved mean Dice scores of 0.7970, 0.8930, and 0.8986 across the small, medium, and large groups.
SAM ViT-B showed a different pattern, with corresponding scores of 0.5573, 0.5460, and 0.2295.
The observed results therefore do not support a general statement that smaller polyps were more
difficult for every evaluated model. Other image properties and the relative extent of the fixed-pixel
box prompt may contribute to the group differences.

Boundary visibility proxy analysis
Boundary visibility was explored using the colour separation between narrow foreground and
background bands adjacent to the ground-truth contour. This image-derived measure was treated as
a proxy rather than an expert annotation of weak boundaries. In the CVC colour-contrast groups,
SAMed achieved mean boundary F1 scores of 0.4787 in the lowest quartile and 0.5617 in the highest
quartile. However, the relationship was not uniform across methods and outcomes. SAM ViT-B had
lower mean Dice in the high-contrast group despite a higher boundary F1 score.
Relative area and the contrast proxy were correlated, with Spearman coefficients of 0.2574 on Kvasir
and 0.3707 on CVC. Crossed size and contrast tables and alternative band widths were therefore
examined descriptively. The results remain dependent on the operational proxy and do not establish
an independent causal effect of boundary visibility. Further validation with expert boundary
assessments would be needed before interpreting these groups as clinically defined weak-boundary
cases.

Sensitivity to box extent
The existing fixed-expansion evaluations showed that prompt extent affected the recorded
segmentation results. PP-SAM obtained its highest mean Dice among the four tested settings at an
expansion of 10 pixels on both datasets. On CVC, its mean Dice decreased from 0.8948 at 10 pixels
to 0.8025 at 50 pixels. This comparison concerns deterministic outward expansion of ground-truth-
derived boxes; it does not evaluate displaced user prompts, point prompts, or automatic lesion
localisation.

object sizes Object scale was defined as the fraction of image pixels occupied by the ground-truth foreground. Images were grouped into small (at most 5%), medium (more than 5% and at most 20%), and large (more than 20%) foreground regions. The Kvasir validation groups contained 29, 61, and 30 images, respectively, while the corresponding CVC groups contained 229, 321, and 62 images. These categories describe relative image area rather than physical lesion dimensions. The relationship between scale and overlap accuracy differed across models. On CVC, PP-SAM achieved mean Dice scores of 0.7970, 0.8930, and 0.8986 across the small, medium, and large groups. SAM ViT-B showed a different pattern, with corresponding scores of 0.5573, 0.5460, and 0.2295. The observed results therefore do not support a general statement that smaller polyps were more difficult for every evaluated model. Other image properties and the relative extent of the fixed-pixel box prompt may contribute to the group differences. Boundary visibility proxy analysis Boundary visibility was explored using the colour separation between narrow foreground and background bands adjacent to the ground-truth contour. This image-derived measure was treated as a proxy rather than an expert annotation of weak boundaries. In the CVC colour-contrast groups, SAMed achieved mean boundary F1 scores of 0.4787 in the lowest quartile and 0.5617 in the highest quartile. However, the relationship was not uniform across methods and outcomes. SAM ViT-B had lower mean Dice in the high-contrast group despite a higher boundary F1 score. Relative area and the contrast proxy were correlated, with Spearman coefficients of 0.2574 on Kvasir and 0.3707 on CVC. Crossed size and contrast tables and alternative band widths were therefore examined descriptively. The results remain dependent on the operational proxy and do not establish an independent causal effect of boundary visibility. Further validation with expert boundary assessments would be needed before interpreting these groups as clinically defined weak-boundary cases. Sensitivity to box extent The existing fixed-expansion evaluations showed that prompt extent affected the recorded segmentation results. PP-SAM obtained its highest mean Dice among the four tested settings at an expansion of 10 pixels on both datasets. On CVC, its mean Dice decreased from 0.8948 at 10 pixels to 0.8025 at 50 pixels. This comparison concerns deterministic outward expansion of ground-truth- derived boxes; it does not evaluate displaced user prompts, point prompts, or automatic lesion localisation. 6 复现入口与证据状态 新增分析协议：analysis/PROTOCOL.md。执行脚本：analysis/complete_supplement.py。所有 原始图像、模型权重、正式 summary 和 per-image 文件均作为只读输入；结果写入本项目 analysis/outputs。 输出 用途 image_features.csv 732 条图像面积、对比度、梯度及分组 per_image_joined.csv 5856 条正式指标与新增表面指标 scale_groups.csv / scale_training_tertiles.csv 主要尺度表与训练分位数敏感性 boundary_*_sensitivity.csv 带宽及梯度定义敏感性 scale_contrast_crossed.csv 尺度与对比度交叉分组 prediction_audit.csv / summary_reconciliation.csv 预测来源匹配及正式均值复核 prompt_expansion_curves.csv 可追溯的框外扩曲线数据 provenance.json 输入路径、SHA256、软件与阈值记录 复现命令：使用现有 sam_env 的 Python 运行 analysis/complete_supplement.py。生成 Word 报 告需使用 Codex 工作区依赖中的 Python 运行 analysis/build_supplement_report.py。提示工具 包的完整命令见 tools/README.md。 现在可以如何表述 目标尺度：已补充系统的图像相对面积分组描述性分析。弱边界：已补充操作定义明确的图 像对比度／梯度探索分析及表面指标，尚无临床或专家弱边界标签。提示质量：已有固定外 扩敏感性研究，不宣称覆盖所有提示方式。工具包：已整理为可执行、可复用的标注派生提 示脚本与说明，仍需模型特定接入。 这轮补充不改变多种子、显著性检验、独立测试和临床验证尚未完成的状态，也不构成 SOTA 或部署效果证明。 7

研究叙述与范围
 课题名称：Promptable Medical Image Segmentation Foundation Models for Endoscopic
 Polyp Segmentation。

 本文研究在真实掩膜生成的框提示下，SAM 系列模型如何完成息肉分割，以及息肉领
 域适配、标注预算与框外扩范围如何改变其表现。正文按零样本参照、领域适配、标
 注预算和提示敏感性展开，分别报告 Kvasir 验证与 CVC 跨数据集结果。
 当前证据支持复现、适配与比较分析的贡献定位。PP-SAM 作为已有方法的全参对照；
 原始实现与实验记录用于核实各方法的训练范围。比较采用共同的数据划分和评估框
 规则，但保留模型预处理与训练方案差异。

四个研究问题

 RQ1 在默认框提示条件下，零样本 SAM 与 MedSAM 的表现如何？

 RQ2 使用完整 Kvasir 训练集进行领域适配后，各方案在 Kvasir 验证集与 CVC 上的表
 现如何？

 RQ3 SAMed 和 PP-SAM 在 1、5、10 及 880 张训练图像下呈现怎样的性能变化？
 RQ4 对已评估的适配模型，固定框外扩范围如何影响两个数据集上的分割表现？

贡献表述与证据边界

 贡献拟表述为：实现并整理 SAM 系列模型的息肉适配与评估流程；在明确划分和提示
 条件下完成比较；分析所记录运行中的标注预算与框外扩敏感性。暂不声称原创架构、
 SOTA、统计显著性、自动检测能力或已完成系统尺度与弱边界分析。

3 Research Methodology

3.1 Study design

    This study evaluates SAM-based models for endoscopic polyp segmentation and examines how
    their performance changes with polyp-specific adaptation, annotation budget, and the spatial
    extent of the box prompt. The experimental comparisons address four questions: how zero-shot
    SAM and MedSAM perform under the selected evaluation conditions; how polyp-specific
    adaptation changes performance within and across datasets; how the evaluated adaptation
    recipes behave with different numbers of labelled training images; and how fixed outward box
    expansion affects segmentation accuracy. The comparisons are descriptive. They are used to
    characterise the evaluated models and training recipes rather than to establish a universal
    ranking of adaptation methods.
    The data partition separates model fitting from validation. Kvasir-SEG provides 880 training
    images and 120 validation images, and the supplied filename lists have no overlap. Adaptation
    uses images from the training partition, including the smaller support sets used for the few-shot
    experiments. The validation partition is also used to select checkpoints during training. Its
    reported scores therefore describe performance on the model-selection validation set, rather
    than on an independent test set. CVC-ClinicDB, comprising 612 images, provides the cross-
    dataset evaluation set. The two evaluation sets are reported separately so that performance on
    the development dataset can be distinguished from performance on another dataset.
    The first comparison establishes the zero-shot reference using SAM with ViT-B and ViT-L
    backbones and MedSAM. The second comparison evaluates polyp-specific adaptation using
    SAMed LoRA, MedSAM with LoRA, and Medical SAM Adapter with ViT-B and ViT-L
    backbones. PP-SAM is included as an existing full-parameter adaptation reference. These
    methods differ in their initial weights, trainable components, and training procedures. A shared
    evaluation partition and prompt-generation rule improve the interpretability of their results, but
    do not isolate the causal effect of any single architectural or optimisation choice.
    Implementation and training details are therefore reported for each method.
    The annotation-budget comparison uses the available SAMed and PP-SAM runs with 1, 5, 10,
    and 880 labelled training images. The smaller support sets are drawn from the Kvasir training
    partition, while the full-data setting uses all 880 training images. This comparison examines the
    observed relationship between annotation budget and performance for the recorded runs. It does
    not assume that performance increases monotonically with the number of images. Because
    repeated training seeds and repeated support-set draws are not available, differences between
    budgets are interpreted as outcomes of the evaluated runs, not as estimates of the expected
    performance over all possible training samples.
                                                                                                    5
Evaluation prompts are generated from the ground-truth masks. A tight bounding box is
expanded outward by a fixed distance in original-image pixels and clipped to the image
boundary. The main comparison uses an expansion of 30 pixels. Additional evaluations use
expansions of 0, 10, 30, and 50 pixels for selected trained models. This procedure changes box
extent deterministically; it does not simulate random displacement of a user-provided box. The
results consequently describe segmentation conditional on access to a ground-truth-derived
location prompt. They do not measure the performance of a fully automatic detection and
segmentation system.
Dice and intersection over union quantify mask overlap, with precision, recall, and HD95
providing complementary descriptions of foreground prediction and boundary error. Where
reported, the standard deviation describes variation across evaluation images rather than
variation across training seeds. The main comparisons are restricted to models receiving the
prescribed box prompts. Methods evaluated without those prompts are presented separately
because they use different input information. Together, these design choices allow the thesis to
relate the observed results to their data, training, and prompt conditions while keeping the
conclusions within the available evidence.

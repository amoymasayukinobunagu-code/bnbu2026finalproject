polyp-specific adaptation, annotation budget, and the spatial extent of the box prompt. The experimental comparisons address four questions: how zero-shot SAM and MedSAM perform under the selected evaluation conditions; how polyp-specific adaptation changes performance within and across datasets; how the evaluated adaptation recipes behave with different numbers of labelled training images; and how fixed outward box expansion affects segmentation accuracy. The comparisons are descriptive. They are used to characterise the evaluated models and training recipes rather than to establish a universal ranking of adaptation methods. The data partition separates model fitting from validation. Kvasir-SEG provides 880 training images and 120 validation images, and the supplied filename lists have no overlap. Adaptation uses images from the training partition, including the smaller support sets used for the few-shot experiments. The validation partition is also used to select checkpoints during training. Its reported scores therefore describe performance on the model-selection validation set, rather than on an independent test set. CVC-ClinicDB, comprising 612 images, provides the cross- dataset evaluation set. The two evaluation sets are reported separately so that performance on the development dataset can be distinguished from performance on another dataset. The first comparison establishes the zero-shot reference using SAM with ViT-B and ViT-L backbones and MedSAM. The second comparison evaluates polyp-specific adaptation using SAMed LoRA, MedSAM with LoRA, and Medical SAM Adapter with ViT-B and ViT-L backbones. PP-SAM is included as an existing full-parameter adaptation reference. These methods differ in their initial weights, trainable components, and training procedures. A shared evaluation partition and prompt-generation rule improve the interpretability of their results, but do not isolate the causal effect of any single architectural or optimisation choice. Implementation and training details are therefore reported for each method. The annotation-budget comparison uses the available SAMed and PP-SAM runs with 1, 5, 10, and 880 labelled training images. The smaller support sets are drawn from the Kvasir training partition, while the full-data setting uses all 880 training images. This comparison examines the observed relationship between annotation budget and performance for the recorded runs. It does not assume that performance increases monotonically with the number of images. Because repeated training seeds and repeated support-set draws are not available, differences between budgets are interpreted as outcomes of the evaluated runs, not as estimates of the expected performance over all possible training samples. 5 Evaluation prompts are generated from the ground-truth masks. A tight bounding box is expanded outward by a fixed distance in original-image pixels and clipped to the image boundary. The main comparison uses an expansion of 30 pixels. Additional evaluations use expansions of 0, 10, 30, and 50 pixels for selected trained models. This procedure changes box extent deterministically; it does not simulate random displacement of a user-provided box. The results consequently describe segmentation conditional on access to a ground-truth-derived location prompt. They do not measure the performance of a fully automatic detection and segmentation system. Dice and intersection over union quantify mask overlap, with precision, recall, and HD95 providing complementary descriptions of foreground prediction and boundary error. Where reported, the standard deviation describes variation across evaluation images rather than variation across training seeds. The main comparisons are restricted to models receiving the prescribed box prompts. Methods evaluated without those prompts are presented separately because they use different input information. Together, these design choices allow the thesis to relate the observed results to their data, training, and prompt conditions while keeping the conclusions within the available evidence. 6 写作核验与后续工作 本页为审阅记录，不纳入最终论文章节。样章依据本地项目材料撰写，尚未登记为作 者已核验的最终文本。样章没有引用外部研究结论；正式介绍各方法时将补入经原文 核验的引用。 本轮已核对 数据划分 训练列表 880 项、验证列表 120 项，集合交集为 0；正式主线 summary 文件 中的数据集数量与上述评估数量相符。 实验定位 主线模型、少样本设置和框外扩范围依据正式数字索引、消融记录及结果 文件整理。Kvasir 验证参与权重选择的表述有训练代码和记录支持。 证据来 源 主 要 入 口 为 charter/PROJECT_BRIEF.md 、 charter/SCOPE.md 、 numbers/NUMBERS_INDEX.md 、 numbers/official_core/ 及 numbers/prompt_ablation_box_expand_SUMMARY.md。详细对应关系保存在本稿同名 的来源清单中。 后续写作前核实 训练与推理细节 逐模型核实初始化、可训练参数、损失、支持集清单、训练配置、 候选掩膜选择及阈值。当前代码可能含后续改动，需与正式运行记录对应。 指标与比较条件 核实 HD95 单位和空掩膜规则、各模型预处理及输出恢复流程；FPS 尚不用于统一效率结论。 图表与引用 从正式结果生成曲线；定性图使用对应权重预测。核验现有 34 条 BibTeX 及其正文论点，避免仅凭搜索摘要确认引用。 特殊来源 区分 SAMed 主表与本机消融的 box30 结果；MedSAM+LoRA 的 CVC box0/10/50 为用户提供数值，没有本地原始 JSON。 建议审阅重点 优先审阅四个研究问题是否符合项目定位，以及 3.1 节的英文表达和技术深度。结构与 风格确定后，继续补写第 3 章的数据、提示与评估部分，再完成第 4 章和第 5 章。 7

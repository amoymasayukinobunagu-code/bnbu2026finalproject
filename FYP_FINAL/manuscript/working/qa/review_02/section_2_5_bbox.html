RQ1 asks how zero-shot SAM and MedSAM perform on the Kvasir-SEG validation split and CVC-ClinicDB under a common box-prompt protocol. RQ2 asks how models adapted with LoRA or adapters on the full Kvasir-SEG training split compare with the zero-shot baselines on those datasets. RQ3 explores how performance varies with prompt type, box expansion and box translation, and across groups defined by relative polyp area and boundary contrast. The data partition separates model fitting from validation. Kvasir-SEG provides 880 training images and 120 validation images, and the supplied filename lists have no overlap. Adaptation uses images from the training partition, including the smaller support sets used for the few-shot experiments. The validation partition is also used to select checkpoints during training. Its reported scores therefore describe performance on the model-selection validation set, rather than on an independent test set. CVC-ClinicDB, comprising 612 images, provides the cross- dataset evaluation set. The two evaluation sets are reported separately so that performance on the development dataset can be distinguished from performance on another dataset. The first comparison establishes the zero-shot reference using SAM with ViT-B and ViT-L backbones and MedSAM. The second comparison evaluates polyp-specific adaptation using SAMed LoRA, MedSAM with LoRA, and Medical SAM Adapter with ViT-B and ViT-L backbones. PP-SAM is included as an existing full-parameter adaptation reference. These methods differ in their initial weights, trainable components, and training procedures. A shared evaluation partition and prompt-generation rule improve the interpretability of their results, but do not isolate the causal effect of any single architectural or optimisation choice. Implementation and training details are therefore reported for each method. A supplementary analysis of RQ2 compares recorded SAMed and PP-SAM runs with 1, 5, 10, and 880 labelled training images. A separate short-budget SAMed 5-shot pilot examines variation across training seeds and support draws. The formal few-shot comparisons use the recorded checkpoints for each budget, while the pilot uses its own fixed training schedule. 5 The default prompt is a tight ground-truth box expanded outward by 30 pixels in the original image. Selected trained models were also evaluated with fixed expansions of 0, 10, 30, and 50 pixels. The point and box-translation experiments use SAM ViT-B and MSA-B with the same image-level prompts. Translations preserve box dimensions and are limited by the available space at the image edge. On CVC-ClinicDB, paired changes in Dice are also summarised by video sequence. For the image-condition analysis, relative polyp area is measured as the fraction of image pixels covered by the ground-truth mask. Boundary contrast is estimated from the colour difference between narrow regions inside and outside the annotated contour. A grayscale gradient measure provides a sensitivity check. The analysis uses these image-derived proxies for boundary visibility. Dice and intersection over union measure mask overlap, while boundary F1 and surface HD95 describe contour agreement and distance. All distance measurements are expressed in pixels at the original image resolution. 6 写作核验与后续工作 本页为审阅记录，不纳入最终论文章节。样章依据本地项目材料撰写，尚未登记为作 者已核验的最终文本。样章没有引用外部研究结论；正式介绍各方法时将补入经原文 核验的引用。 本轮已核对 数据划分 训练列表 880 项、验证列表 120 项，集合交集为 0；正式主线 summary 文件 中的数据集数量与上述评估数量相符。 实验定位 主线模型、少样本设置和框外扩范围依据正式数字索引、消融记录及结果 文件整理。Kvasir 验证参与权重选择的表述有训练代码和记录支持。 证据来源 官方范围见课题说明。提示、尺度和边界的新增实验以分析总记录为准。 详细对应关系保存在本稿的来源清单中。 后续写作前核实 训练与推理细节 逐模型核实初始化、可训练参数、损失、支持集清单、训练配置、 候选掩膜选择及阈值。当前代码可能含后续改动，需与正式运行记录对应。 指标与比较条件 主表采用重算的原图像素表面 HD95。FPS 按统一测速记录单列， 覆盖 SAM-B、SAM-L 和 MSA-B。 图表与引用 从正式结果生成曲线；定性图使用对应权重预测。核验现有 34 条 BibTeX 及其正文论点，避免仅凭搜索摘要确认引用。 特殊来源 区分 SAMed 主表与本机消融的 box30 结果；MedSAM+LoRA 的 CVC box0/10/50 为用户提供数值，没有本地原始 JSON。 建议审阅重点 本轮已按确认的三个问题同步修改首页、第 1、3、5、6 章提纲和 3.1 英文样章。后 续核实章节中的具体实现和模型来源。 7
